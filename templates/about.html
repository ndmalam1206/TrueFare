About TrueFare

Making Local Transportation Fair, Transparent & Trustworthy.

TrueFare is a smart local transport fare verification platform designed to help everyday travelers understand the expected fare before starting their journey.

Local fares, made easier to understand

Passengers using auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, shared tempos and other local shared transport often struggle to know the reasonable fare for a particular route.

TrueFare aims to solve this problem by bringing local fare information, route details and community feedback together in one simple platform.

The Problem We're Solving

  • Passengers often don't know the expected local fare.
  • Unfamiliar routes can make fare negotiation difficult.
  • Different fare demands can create confusion.
  • Reliable local fare information is not always easily available.

Our Solution – How TrueFare Works

1

Select your starting point and destination.

2

Check available local fare information.

3

View administrator-verified fares or clearly labelled community-based estimates.

4

Explore the route on the map.

5

Compare the driver's demanded fare with available fare information.

6

Submit feedback to help improve local fare information.

What Makes TrueFare Different?

  • Local fare information.
  • Transparent fare comparison.
  • Map-based route visualization.
  • Community contributions.
  • Administrator-reviewed feedback.
  • Simple and accessible user experience.

Our Commitment to Transparency

Verified Fare: Fare information entered or approved by the administrator.

Community Estimate: An indicative estimate based on available user feedback.

Unavailable Information: When reliable data is insufficient, the platform communicates that clearly.

TrueFare is not a ride-booking application and does not use dynamic pricing. It helps users make informed decisions using available local fare information.

Who Can Benefit?

Daily commuters, students, tourists, new residents, families and everyday local transport passengers.

Our Mission

To make local transportation more transparent by helping passengers access understandable and reliable fare information before travelling.

Our Vision

To build a trusted local transport fare information network that can gradually expand across cities and regions in India.
